Field reference
JSON array. Options: {symbol, type:"put"|"call", side:"short"|"long", strike, expiration:"YYYY-MM-DD", qty, credit|cost, currentPrice, underlyingPrice}. Stock: {symbol, type:"stock", qty, costBasis, underlyingPrice}. Optional watchlist array for candidate screening: bare tickers ["AMD","COST"], or enriched objects {symbol, list, price, ivr, liquidity (1-4★), beta, earningsDays, yearHigh, yearLow, etf}. Each candidate is scored against the entry rules (liquidity ≥3★, IVR>30 to sell premium, no earnings within 45 DTE, no long near 52-wk highs, short only ETFs near lows) and given a structure recommendation; missing fields show as "?" and are flagged to confirm manually. Strategy grouping: option legs sharing an underlying and expiration are analyzed as one strategy (vertical, strangle, iron condor, …), so risk and buying power reflect the combined structure — a defined-risk spread uses width − net credit, not naked margin on each leg; a short call covered by held shares waives its margin. Deployment: Total deployed = Net Liq − Option BP (equals your broker's BP Usage), shown as a % of Net Liq. Per-bucket = estimated BP used ÷ Net Liq. Buckets: stock = ~50% Reg-T margin of market value; premium selling = net-credit structures with a short leg (defined-risk = width − credit; naked = ~20% of underlying notional, a rough Reg-T-style estimate, not your broker's figure); moon shots = net-debit / long-only structures, capital = debit paid. Total deployment cap is set by /VX (rulebook): ≥25 → deploy up to 50% of total buying power; 20-25 → 45%; 18-20 → 40%; below 18 → 30%. The stock/premium/moon-shot mix percentages above apply to that deployed (allocated) portion, not total buying power. CSV upload: a header row plus columns like Symbol, Type, Call/Put, Quantity (negative = short), Strike Price, Exp Date, Trade Price, Mark, Underlying Last Price. The Call/Put column wins over a generic Type of "Equity Option"; OCC option symbols (e.g. "AAPL 260711P00180000") are decoded automatically; rows with no quantity (broker "Total" lines) are skipped. The parsed result lands in the box above for review — nothing leaves this page.
